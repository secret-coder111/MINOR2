Your privacy boundary

  • Passwords and typed field values are never read.
  • Page structure is analysed on your device.
  • Saved corrections contain origins and coarse context.
  • Reputation lookup is optional and off by default.

PhishGuard explains indicators. It does not verify a website's owner or guarantee safety.

02

Optional reputation service

Local analysis only

When enabled, the hostname of each inspected page or embedded frame is sent to your configured service. Passwords, field values, page content, URL paths and queries are not sent.

Endpoint format and privacy

Use HTTPS (HTTP is allowed for localhost development). The endpoint must return JSON such as {"verdict":"malicious"}. A remote service can observe your IP address and requested hostnames.

03

Review saved corrections

Corrections help you investigate wrong results. They do not automatically change predictions or become verified training labels.

What stays on this device?

Page results last only for this browser session and are removed when tabs close. Up to 100 corrections stay on this device. They retain the top-page and evidence origins, expected and observed outcomes, time, policy version, and coarse inspection context. No full URL, page text or field values are retained.

Check the actual page and its legitimate ownership before marking a correction reviewed. Reviewed labels remain candidates for an independently verified benchmark; they are not automatic training data or site allowlists.
