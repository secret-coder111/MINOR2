Why isn't every external destination phishing?

A college may use an authorized identity provider. A typical federated flow sends you to the provider's genuine sign-in page and returns an assertion or token to the app. It does not mean every app should send your password to an arbitrary external form.

App → genuine identity provider → authorized return. Check the actual provider domain and whether the flow is expected. Cross-site movement alone is inconclusive.
