Get ready

Present it in three steps

  1. Load and refresh.
    Reload PhishGuard in your browser's Extensions page, then refresh this tab. Keep reputation disabled for the local walkthrough.

  2. Change one signal.
    Use the interactive test to move from Ordinary login to External destination, then Brand + external destination.

  3. Show the real result.
    Allow a moment for rescanning. Open the popup, explain the reasons, and choose Ordinary login / reset to clear the warning.

Let the examiner control the experiment

Evidence you can inspect, not a result you have to trust.

  1. Open any of the three pages. Expand its actual title and form destination.
  2. Choose the order yourself. Read the real extension popup, not the page's expected label.
  3. Change the harmless page's title or form action in browser DevTools. Use Check again, then restore it and compare.
  4. Use independently hosted public test pages too. A browser blocklist warning and PhishGuard's own verdict are separate observations.

A controlled fixture demonstrates cause and effect. An independent unseen benchmark is still required to measure real-world accuracy.
